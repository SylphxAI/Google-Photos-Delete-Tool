Using the tool safely

The tool moves the photos you choose to Google Photos Trash. Restoration is possible only while items remain in Trash and Google Photos’ current retention policy allows it; no recovery period is guaranteed. "Empty trash afterwards" is optional, asks for confirmation and cannot be undone. Empty Trash and any permanent deletion cannot be undone. If Trash is full, Google may prompt permanent deletion instead. Run a dry run first and keep backups of photos you value. Google can change Google Photos at any time. When a change breaks the tool, it stops rather than guessing, and we work to fix it quickly.

To restore eligible items, open Google Photos Trash, select the photos or videos and choose Restore. See Google’s desktop delete guidance and desktop restore guidance (checked 2026-10-29) for the current conditions. The tool cannot restore permanently deleted items.
