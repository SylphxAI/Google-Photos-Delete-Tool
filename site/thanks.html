Thank you for buying Pro

Thank you. Your Pro token is shown on the checkout success page and emailed to the address you paid with. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive.

Where to paste it

  1. Open the extension (or the userscript panel) in Google Photos.
  2. Find the Pro license box.
  3. Paste the token and press Activate.

Pro works offline and does not expire. Lost your token? Recover it at buy.sylphx.com/recover, or write to hi@sylphx.com with the email you paid with.
