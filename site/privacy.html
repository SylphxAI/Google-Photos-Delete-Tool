The extension

The extension runs only on photos.google.com, inside your browser. Bulk Delete, Find duplicates and the Blurry / Low-Res detector run 100% locally: your photos, thumbnails, fingerprints and sharpness scores never leave your device, and there are zero photo uploads. The extension has no analytics and no telemetry. A Pro token is checked offline on your device using public keys shipped with the client and is never sent to us. The client makes no JWKS or entitlement request; a new signing key requires a client update. Build maintainers refresh the public-key snapshot from Money’s environment-specific public JWKS without any token, claim or photo information; if you use your browser's sync account, your browser keeps it on your other signed-in devices.

AI 4K Restore (optional)

AI 4K Restore is the only feature that sends a photo, and it sends one photo at a time. Only when you click AI 4K Restore on a single photo and confirm the transfer notice is that photo sent over HTTPS to Sylphx AI (sylphx.ai). Sylphx servers process it ephemerally in memory, with zero persistent storage or retention: no copy is kept after the 4K result is returned to your browser. To render the image, Sylphx uses a paid third-party AI inference API whose terms for this request exclude training AI models on it (trains: false); any short-term processing on the provider's side is governed by its API terms. The restored image is saved only if you download it.

To show and charge your credits, the extension sends your Sylphx wallet token, never a photo, to Sylphx when the studio opens and when you confirm a restore. We keep a ledger of credit purchases and use (wallet id, amounts, dates) to deliver what you paid for.

Before your first purchase, Pro activation or restore, and whenever the Terms or this notice change, you are asked to tick a box and agree. That acceptance record (an id, the two version dates, the time and where you agreed) is saved in your browser. When you connect a Sylphx wallet or account, it is also sent to Sylphx (api.sylphx.com) so your account’s agreement is on file. It carries no photo or library data.
