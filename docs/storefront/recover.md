# Recover your Pro licence

Enter the email address used at checkout. If a paid licence exists, Money sends
an expiring recovery link. The response never says whether that address has a
purchase, and no licence token is shown to an unverified browser.

Open the recovery link within one hour to view and copy your licence token.
Then open the extension (or the userscript panel) in Google Photos, find the
**Pro license** box, paste the token and press **Activate**. It is checked on
your device and works offline.

Existing licences keep their original product, plan, seats and expiry. Do not
share a licence token or recovery link in a public issue, log or repository.
