# Free for good. Pro when you clean up often.

The delete engine, dry run, duplicate finding and Empty Trash are free forever.
Pro adds convenience for people who clean up a large library repeatedly:

- Type filters: screenshots, videos, photos, animations or collages.
- Date filters before, after or between two dates.
- Up to 20 saved presets for repeated cleanups.
- Duplicate keep rules, auto-accept groups that are 98% or more alike, and CSV export.
- Dry-run reports with CSV export.
- Storage Saver presets for screenshots, videos and screenshots over a year old.

Original file sizes are not exposed by Google Photos tiles, so Storage Saver
reports the matching count, not an invented GB estimate. Trash is recoverable;
moving photos to Trash does not immediately free storage.
